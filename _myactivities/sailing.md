Living in Santa Barbara, it would be a shame not to spend time on the water. Most of my sailing these days
is racing in the Santa Barbara Yacht Club's Wet Wednesday series: an evening race every Wednesday, from the
start of daylight saving time well into the autumn. The start line is right off the club, and the fleet
ranges from big boats down to a one-design fleet of Harbor 20s. I am lucky to be able to race a Harbor 20!


The Harbor 20
---

The [Harbor 20](https://www.harbor20.org) is a small one-design keelboat. It was conceived by a group of sailors
at Newport Harbor Yacht Club and designed by Steve Schock; W. D. Schock Corp. has built it in California since 1997.

* 20 ft (6.1 m) long, 7 ft (2.1 m) wide
* About 1,800 lb (820 kg), half of it lead in a bulb keel
* About 220 sq ft (20 m²) of sail

It is a stable, forgiving boat for two or three people, which makes it ideal for an evening race after work.
Strict one-design class rules keep all boats the same, so the results come down to the crew rather than the
budget.


Wet Wednesday race tracks
---

I record every race with a GPS watch. Since 2019, the tracks of more than 150 races have come together on
the **[Wet Wednesday race tracks](/sba_wet_wednesday/)** page.
