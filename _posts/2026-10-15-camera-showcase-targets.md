A new camera with a 26′ × 26′ field of view and good sensitivity from SDSS u to Y needs some first-light pictures that show what it can do. I put together an interactive list of candidate fields: **[Camera showcase targets](/camera-showcase/)**.

The list has three groups:

- **Galaxies that fit the field**, including edge-on dust lanes (NGC 891, Centaurus A) and galaxies hidden behind the Milky Way (Maffei 1 and 2, IC 342, Circinus).
- **Star-forming galaxies** where the u band picks out young stars and HII regions (NGC 604 in M33, M101, 30 Doradus, NGC 346).
- **Galactic dust clouds** where Y-band imaging sees through dust that is opaque at optical wavelengths (Barnard 68, B335, the Pillars of Creation, the Carina Keyhole).

Each field has a 40′ finding chart in u, g, r, i, z and Y from public surveys, with the camera footprint drawn in. The page also shows how many dark hours per month each field is above 30° from the LCO 1-m sites in the northern and southern hemispheres. You can filter by month, hemisphere and target type, and download the list as CSV.

The page and its survey-cutout pipeline were built with an AI coding assistant. The image credits for each survey are listed at the bottom of the page.
